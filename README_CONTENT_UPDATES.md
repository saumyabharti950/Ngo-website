## Source reviewed

## Updates completed

- Replaced the previous Bharat Shakti Foundation identity with **Social Initiative for India Foundation (SIFI Foundation)** throughout the live page.
- Replaced the generic charity/donation hero with the approved SIFI positioning: “Creating Opportunities. Strengthening Communities.”
- Removed unverified claims: tax-exemption badges, donation targets and amounts, donor counts, project/community/lives figures, campaign locations and “active” fundraisers.
- Replaced the impact-number strip with the approved four homepage themes: multi-sector development, evidence-based approach, collaborative development and community-centric action.
- Rebuilt the feature cards around the approved priority areas: public health, education, skills/livelihoods and sustainability.
- Replaced fundraising campaign cards with SIFI’s delivery model: understand needs, build partnerships, deliver and measure.
- Added complete sections for About, Vision, Mission, Values, Areas of Work, Approach, CSR Partnerships, Research, Impact, Leadership, Get Involved, Transparency, Contact and legal-policy summaries.
- Updated header navigation, calls to action and footer links so they scroll to the corresponding live sections.
- Updated contact details to the approved Ranchi registered office, `info@sififoundation.org`, `projects@sififoundation.org` and `0651-3591618`.
- Added the Section 8 not-for-profit designation from the supplied content.

## Before public launch

The policy document advises not to publish legal registrations, 80G/12A/FCRA or CSR-1 claims, bank/payment details, donation tax benefits, beneficiary figures, funding amounts, district coverage or impact percentages unless currently verified. Those claims are deliberately not included in this update.

The supplied policy framework is included as expandable web summaries. Before publishing, it can be split into individual legal pages if separate URLs are required.
